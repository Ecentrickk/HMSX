## 📊 WHAT H1MS DOES (Summary)

H1MS is a **complete hospital management system** with:

- ✅ **Patient Management** - Register, track, manage patients
- ✅ **Appointment Scheduling** - Book appointments with auto-reminders
- ✅ **Doctor Module** - Prescriptions, reports, digital signatures
- ✅ **Nurse Module** - Vital signs, medication tracking, alerts
- ✅ **Receptionist Module** - Front desk, billing, appointments
- ✅ **Pharmacy Module** - Inventory management, stock tracking
- ✅ **Billing System** - Charges, payments, financial reports
- ✅ **Admin Dashboard** - Users, audit logs, system control
- ✅ **Notifications** - Email, SMS, WhatsApp, real-time alerts
- ✅ **Role-Based Access** - 5 roles with 55+ permissions
- ✅ **Security** - JWT auth, password hashing, audit trails
- ✅ **Scalability** - Handles 50-100+ concurrent users
